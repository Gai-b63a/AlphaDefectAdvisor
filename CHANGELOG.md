# Changelog

Player-facing changes are listed by mod version, newest first.

## 0.2.8

- The advice panel follows the game language across all 17 language entries in Slay the Spire 2 v0.114.0. Percentages use local formatting, and Arabic text reads right to left.
- Ranked choices appear first. A separate estimate then shows the chance of clearing the entire run. It uses comparable runs from your local history. When history is limited on A10, the panel labels the AI floor average separately.

## 0.2.7

- The README and Steam Workshop description are available in English. The in-game panel in this version still uses Japanese.

## 0.2.6

- When a potion reward appears and your belt is full, the advisor can name the potion and slot to discard, suggest drinking a potion, or recommend leaving the reward.

## 0.2.5

- Added advice for more card choices, including hand selections, combat piles, deck removal, transformation, enchantment, and card bundles. For multi-card choices, the advisor reevaluates the remaining cards after each pick.

## 0.2.4

- Added recommendations for cards offered by Discovery, including the option to skip, and for the card to upgrade when smithing at a rest site.

## 0.2.3

- Map advice now reads the visible map before the reward screen underneath it. The panel explains when it is waiting while you browse the map or travel.

## 0.2.2

- Updated the Ironclad advisor for the Slay the Spire 2 v0.114.0 public beta.
- Drag the panel by its header to move it away from relics and other game controls. The panel remembers its position.

## 0.2.1

- Updates target the existing Steam Workshop item so subscribers can keep the same subscription.

## 0.2.0

- Initial Workshop version for Slay the Spire 2 v0.113.1. The in-game overlay recommends actions for solo Ironclad runs using a bundled inference runtime and model.
